Hero and Villain worlds

Each player is either on the hero side or the villain side, decided the first time they join a world (the mhcVillainMode game rule decides it for new players). Defeating characters of the other side gives skill points.

Skill points

When you defeat a hero or villain of the other side, every player who damaged it gets skill points:

points = (sum of the enemy's 5 stats) ÷ 4 × (your damage ÷ enemy max health) × config multiplier

You always get at least 1 point if you dealt damage. For example, defeating Sir Nighteye alone gives about 20 points, and a random villain gives about 2–3 (up to 5). In Creative mode, points are unlimited.

Skill tree

Open the Quirk Note to see your quirk's skill tree. Passives are unlocked from the start. Each skill costs points and needs the skill before it. Skills marked Goal or Challenge are the strongest ones.

Cooldowns

After you use a skill it has a cooldown and cannot be used again until it ends. Cooldowns reset when you respawn.
