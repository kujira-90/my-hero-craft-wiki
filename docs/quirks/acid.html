Acid

Used by: Mina Ashido

Secretes acid from the skin. The strength and dissolving power of the acid can be adjusted.

Passives

  • Acid ①[Acid Gauge] Using the Quirk drains the gauge. You cannot use the Quirk without gauge. It refills over time.
