Blackwhip

Used by: 5th OFA User Daigoro Banjo, Izuku Midoriya

Releases and controls whip-like black tendrils. Use them to capture targets or to move around.

Passives

  • Blackwhip ①[Blackwhip] Blackwhip's reach scales with Quirk Lv.
  • Black Chain[Passive] While charged with the [Fa Jin] Quirk, Blackwhip turns into Black Chain.ND:fajin
