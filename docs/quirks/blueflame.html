Blue Flame

Used by: Dabi<League of Villains>

Produces extremely hot blue flames. Very powerful, but they burn your own body too.

Passives

  • Blue Flame ①[Flames of Obsession] Using the Quirk raises the gauge; going past the max causes self-damage.
  • Jet[Passive] Double-tap Shift to fly. The gauge rises while flying, and you cannot fly once it is full. Press the sprint key while looking up to rise, using more gauge than usual.
  • Flashfire Fist[Passive] While active, fire skills deal more damage, but heat builds up faster.
