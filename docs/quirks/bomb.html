Explosion

Used by: Katsuki Bakugo

Secretes nitroglycerin-like sweat from the palms and detonates it.

Passives

  • Explosion ①[Sweat Gland Gauge] Using the Quirk fills the gauge; when it is full you take self-damage. It drains over time. The maximum scales with Quirk Lv.
  • Explosion ②[Explosive Turbo] Double-tap W/A/S/D to move in that direction with a small blast. Hold Shift and use W Explosion to rise slightly.
