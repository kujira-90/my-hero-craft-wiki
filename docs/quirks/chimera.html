Chimera (Movie Heroes: Rising)

Used by: Chimera

A Quirk combining the powers of many creatures. Fights with a mighty body, and in Serious Mode fires a heat ray from the mouth.

Passives

  • Chimera ①[Apex of Life] Increases attack, movement speed, dash jump power and defense.
  • Serious Mode[Passive] Double-tap Shift to change your form, raising all stats. Uses the Chimera gauge while active and ends at 0. Only usable when the gauge is full.
  • Flight[Passive] Double-tap Shift to fly.
