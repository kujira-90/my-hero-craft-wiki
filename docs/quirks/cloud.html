Cloud

Used by: Oboro Shirakumo

Creates and controls clouds. Ride them or use them as footholds.

Passives

  • Cloud ①[Cloud] Double-tap Shift to summon or put away your cloud. Right-click to ride it, and you can steer while riding. When dismissed, the cooldown depends on the cloud's remaining HP.
  • Cloud ①[Controls] Controls are basically the same as a boat. Look up or down past a certain angle to rise or descend. Up to 3 people can ride, and only the owner can steer the cloud.
