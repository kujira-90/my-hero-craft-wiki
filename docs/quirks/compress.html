Quirks / Villains

Mr. Compress (Villain Union)

Used by: Compression

Compresses anything you touch into a small marble, and restores it whenever you like.

Passives

  • Compress ①[Compressed Marble] Right-click to throw it; its contents come out on impact. Shift + right-click to release them on the spot.
  • Compress ②[Restraint Magic] Entities sealed in a Compressed Marble can do nothing.
  • Compress ③[Magic Show] While compressed yourself, double-tap Shift to break free.
