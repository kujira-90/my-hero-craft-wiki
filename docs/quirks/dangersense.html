Danger Sense

Used by: 4th OFA User Hikage Shinomori, Izuku Midoriya

Senses incoming danger, letting you dodge attacks.

Passives

  • Danger Sense ①[Danger Sense Gauge] When Danger Sense triggers, the gauge fills for a while; it can trigger again once the gauge reaches 0. The gauge amount depends on Quirk Lv.
  • Danger Sense[Passive] While active, automatically dodges attacks.
