Quirks / Villains

Decay

Used by: Tomura Shigaraki<League of Villains>

Decays anything touched with all five fingers. The decay spreads from what you touch.

Passives

  • Decay ①[Decay Effect] When applied, the target takes unblockable damage for a while.
  • Decay ②[Decay Damage] Ignores all status effects and armor to cut HP directly.
  • Decay ③[Spreading Decay] Lets you decay sand, gravel and the like. Touching a decaying block also applies Decay.
