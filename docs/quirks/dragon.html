Quirks / Heroes

Dragon

Used by: Ryukyu

Transforms you into a dragon.

Passives

  • Dragon ①[Every Boy's Dream] The gauge at the bottom right drains while flying or using skills; at 0 you can no longer fly.
  • Dragon Form[Passive] Double-tap Shift to transform into a dragon. In Dragon form, triple-tap to return to normal.
  • Flight[Passive] In Dragon form, double-tap Shift to fly.
  • Color Change[Passive] Click the skill tree icon to change the dragon's color.
