Eagle

Used by: Takahiro(Movie: Two Heroes)

Grants the wings and strength of an eagle.

Passives

  • Eagle ①[King of the Sky] The gauge at the bottom right drains while flying or using skills; at 0 you can no longer fly.
  • Eagle ②[Sharp Talons] The power of the eagle increases attack.
  • Flight[Passive] Double-tap Shift to fly.
