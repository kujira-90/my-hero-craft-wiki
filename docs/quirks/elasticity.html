Elasticity

Used by: Gentle Criminal

Gives elasticity to anything you touch, even the air.

Passives

  • Elasticity ①[Graceful Landing] Reduces fall damage.
  • Elasticity Elasticized objects bounce away anything that touches them, depending on the angle. They slowly return to normal.
