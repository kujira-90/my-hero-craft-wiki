Electrification

Used by: Denki Kaminari

Covers your body in electricity and discharges it. Overuse shorts out your brain.

Passives

  • Electrification ①[Jamming Whey] Using the Quirk uses the electrification gauge. At 0 you are vibrated and cannot move for a while.
