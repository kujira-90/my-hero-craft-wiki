Quirks / U.A. Students

Engine

Used by: Tenya Iida

Run at high speed using the engines in your calves.

Passives

  • Engine ①[Boost] Shortens the time to reach full sprint speed.
  • Engine ②[Fuel Gauge] Using the Quirk drains the fuel gauge, and you cannot use it without fuel. It recovers over time and also with orange juice.
  • Recipro Burst[Passive] Double-tap Shift to activate. While active, you always move forward and sprint faster. Uses gauge; at 0 the Engine is disabled for a while. Cannot be combined with Recipro Turbo.
  • Recipro Turbo[Passive] Triple-tap Shift to activate. While active, you always move forward and sprint much faster. Uses gauge; at 0 the Engine is disabled for a while. Cannot be combined with Recipro Burst.
