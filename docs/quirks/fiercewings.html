Quirks / Heroes

Fierce Wings

Used by: Hawks, All For One

Freely controls each feather. Fly, or launch feathers to attack.

Passives

  • Fierce Wings ①[Wings] The gauge at the bottom right drains while flying or using skills; at 0 you can no longer fly.
  • Fierce Wings ②[Feather Gauge] Using the Quirk drains the feather gauge. It recovers over time. Triple-tap Shift to recall launched feathers.
  • Free in the Sky[Passive] Double-tap Shift to fly.
