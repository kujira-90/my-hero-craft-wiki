Flight

Used by: Captain Celebrity(Vigilantes)

Lets you fly freely through the sky.

Passives

  • Flight ①[Aerodynamic Field] Press the special action key [default: C] to fly. The gauge drains while flying, and at 0 you can no longer fly. It can also reduce attacks against you.
  • Flight[Passive] Double-tap Shift to fly.
