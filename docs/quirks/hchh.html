Half-Cold Half-Hot

Used by: Shoto Todoroki

Freezes with the right side and burns with the left.

Passives

  • Half-Cold Half-Hot ①[Heat and Ice] Using either side fills its gauge and slows the cooldown of the matching skills. The maximum scales with Quirk Lv.
  • Flashfreeze Heatwave[Passive] Using Wildfire Blaze on a large enough block of ice releases a powerful blast of wind.
  • Flashfire Fist[Passive] While active, fire skills deal more damage, but heat builds up faster.
