Infrared

Used by: Kashiko Sekigai

Lets you see infrared light and sense your surroundings by heat.

Passives

  • Infrared[Passive] While active, lets you sense entities in range like thermography.
  • Heat Footprints Heat stays for 30 seconds where others or you walked, visible with Infrared [Passive] and the Holographic Map.
  • Map Zoom While using the Holographic Map, scroll to zoom in and out around yourself.
