Magnetism

Used by: Magne<League of Villains>

Gives people magnetism to pull them together or push them apart.

Passives

  • Magnetism ①[S Pole and N Pole] Like poles repel and opposite poles attract. The Quirk range is up to 10 blocks (varies with Quirk Lv).
  • Magnetism ②[Magnetic Force] Shift + scroll to change the strength of the magnetism.
