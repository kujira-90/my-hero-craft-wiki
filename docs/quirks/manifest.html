Manifest

Used by: Tamaki Amajiki

Manifests the traits of what you have eaten on your body.

Passives

  • Manifest ①[Even the Sun Devourer] Some skills cannot be used unless you have eaten the matching food beforehand. Resets on death or when hunger drops to 3 or below.
  • Copy Ability[Passive] Being hit by certain Quirk skills lets you manifest them.
  • Gem Guard[Passive] Activates automatically if you have eaten crystal. On a perfect guard, manifests crystal and reduces more damage than usual.
  • Manifest: Marlin Manifests a marlin. Normal attacks become Marlin Attacks, extending a marlin forward to attack.
  • Manifest: Clam Manifests clams. Increases attack.
  • Manifest: Cow Manifests a cow's body. Increases movement speed.
  • Manifest: Pineapple Normal attacks become Pineapple Attacks, launching the target in front of you upward with a pineapple.
  • Manifest: Crab Manifests crab claws. Normal attacks become slashes and attack increases.
  • Manifest: Octopus Manifests octopus tentacles. Normal attacks become Tentacle Attacks, extending a tentacle forward to pull in the target.
  • Manifest: Scorpion Manifests a scorpion tail. Normal attacks become Scorpion Attacks; hold to extend the tail and attack.
  • Manifest: Chicken Manifests chicken wings. Negates fall damage; double-tap Shift to fly for a while.
  • Manifest: Crystal Manifests crystal on your hands. Increases attack.
  • Manifest: Wave Motion Manifests Wave Motion. Normal attacks become waves.
  • Manifest: Octopus Mirage Manifests an octopus. Turns you invisible.
  • Manifest: Octopus + Crab Claws Manifests octopus tentacles and crab claws. Normal attacks become Tentacle + Crab Attacks, extending tentacles forward to attack.
  • Manifest: Octopus + Crab Shell Manifests octopus tentacles and a crab shell. Increases attack.
