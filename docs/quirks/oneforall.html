Quirks / U.A. Students

OFA

Used by: All Might, Izuku Midoriya

Strengthens your physical abilities.

Passives

  • One For All ②[Inherited Will] Stores the power of those who inherited it. The more inheritors, the stronger One For All becomes.
  • Inheritance Passes One For All on to another. Once passed on, One For All has a limited number of uses.
  • Full Cowl[Passive] While active, increases attack, mobility and defense, and removes self-damage.
  • Symbol of Peace[Passive] Adapts to OFA's power, negating all OFA self-damage and greatly increasing attack.
  • Air Force[Passive] While active, using certain skills with Shift adds a shockwave to them.
  • Leap[Passive] Double-tap Shift to get ready; release the second tap to leap forward.
