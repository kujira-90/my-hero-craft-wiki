Quirks / Vigilantes

Overclock

Used by: O'Clock(Vigilantes), No.6(Vigilantes)

Accelerates your body's movements.

Passives

  • Overclock ①[Fatigue Gauge] Each use of the Quirk wears you out, making Quirk skill cooldowns longer.
  • Ultimate Focus[Passive] Double-tap Shift to enter the zone, where everything around you looks slowed down. Press again to leave it.
  • Hyper Reaction[Passive] A perfect guard lets you dodge at super speed.
