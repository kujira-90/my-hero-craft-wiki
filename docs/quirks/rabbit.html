Rabbit

Used by: Mirko

A mutant quirk with the traits of a rabbit. Leap around with powerful legs and fight with kicks.

Passives

  • Rabbit ①[Like a Fleeing Hare] Shortens the time to a dash jump, strengthens dash jumps, and increases attack and movement speed.
