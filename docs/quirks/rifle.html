Quirks / Heroes

Rifle

Used by: Lady Nagant

Your hair is a rifle. Shoot distant targets with pinpoint accuracy.

Passives

  • Rifle[Passive] Double-tap Shift to switch to Rifle Mode. In Rifle Mode, strikes become sniping. Ammo in your offhand is used first.
  • Scope[Passive] Press Shift while sniping to look through the scope. Scroll to change the zoom.
