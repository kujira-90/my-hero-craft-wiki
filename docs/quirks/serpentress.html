Quirks / Heroes

Serpentress

Used by: Uwabami

A Quirk that controls the snakes on the head. Extends snakes to bite, and detaches them to control them.

Passives

  • Poison Resistance[Passive] You are resistant to poison.
  • Pit Organ[Passive] Entities near detached snakes are highlighted.
  • Revival[Passive] Dead snakes revive after a while.
  • Snake Control[Explore] A mode where snakes freely explore the area.
  • Snake Control[Wait] A mode where snakes wait in place.
  • Snake Control[Attack] A mode where snakes attack the target.
  • Snake Control[Chase] A mode where snakes chase the target.
  • Snake Control[Follow] A mode where snakes follow you. They do not attack.
  • Snakes[Passive] Each snake is different, and their stats increase with Quirk Lv.
  • Snakes[Pounce] Pounces on the target to attack.
  • Snake[Yellow] HP 30, Attack 3, Speed 0.3, venomous, search range 20.
  • Snake[Purple] HP 10, Attack 1, Speed 0.5, search range 30.
  • Snake[Green] HP 15, Attack 6, Speed 0.2, search range 10.
