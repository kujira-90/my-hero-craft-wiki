Steel

Used by: Tetsutetsu Tetsutetsu

Turns your body into steel.

Passives

  • Steel ①[Steel Gauge] While the Steel Gauge is 1 or more, damage is negated. The gauge decreases by the damage taken. Fire damage below a certain amount is negated.
  • Steel ②[Cold Steel] While steeled, taking ice damage or being in a snowy or icy biome chills you into slightly bluish steel that gives off cold air. PowLv +6. Returns to normal after 30 seconds (60 at Quirk Lv 20), with the color fading gradually.
  • Steel ③[Red-Hot Steel] While steeled, taking fire damage or being in the Nether or lava turns you into red-hot steel. PowLv +6. Returns to normal after 30 seconds (60 at Quirk Lv 20), with the color fading gradually.
