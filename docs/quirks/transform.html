Quirks / Villains

Transform

Used by: Himiko Toga<League of Villains>

Transforms you into anyone whose blood you have drunk.

Passives

  • Transform ①[Undo] Double-tap Shift to undo the transformation.
  • Transform ②[Blood Container] Click, or Shift-click, a bleeding entity to collect its blood. Drink it to transform.
  • For Love[Passive] Lets you use the Quirk and Quirk skills of the person you transformed into.
