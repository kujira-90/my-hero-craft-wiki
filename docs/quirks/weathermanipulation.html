Quirks / Villains

Weather Manipulation

Used by: Nine(Movie: Heroes Rising)

A Quirk that controls the weather at will. Raises lightning and tornadoes and changes the weather in a chosen area. It is so powerful that overusing it hurts your own body.

Passives

  • Passive: Too Strong a Price Each use of the Quirk lowers the gauge. Using the Quirk while it is at 0 damages you.
  • Updraft [Passive] Press Shift twice in a row to wrap yourself in wind and fly.
