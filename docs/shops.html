Shops

Two characters run shops in villages. Pay with emeralds.

Support Workshop

Mei Hatsume

WHEREVillages

How to use

  • Mei Hatsume appears in villages and stays there even when you leave.
  • Right-click her to open Hatsume's Support Workshop (sneaking does a normal interaction).
  • The workshop has two tabs: Costumes and Support items. Pay with emeralds from your inventory.
  • Right-click her while holding a Smartphone to open Hatsume's App Store.

Cafeteria

Lunch Rush

WHEREVillages

How to use

  • Lunch Rush appears in villages and stays there even when you leave.
  • Right-click him to open Lunch Rush's Cafeteria.
  • He can cook any food, including food from other mods. The menu has three tabs: U.A. Specials, Classics and Others.
  • Choose how many you want. Hold Shift to change the amount by 8.

Prices

1 : 1one emerald for each hunger point the food restores
