Smartphone

Run a hero agency or villain group, call your allies and learn new moves from HeroTube.

Getting one

64emeralds at Mei Hatsume's workshop (Support tab)

Right-click with the smartphone to open it. The Agency app is installed from the start. Other apps are bought from Hatsume's App Store.

Agency / Villain group

  • Create a Hero Agency (hero side) or a Villain Group (villain side) and give it a name.
  • To invite a player, both of you hold a smartphone and you right-click them.
  • To recruit a character as an ally, right-click an ally-side character that is under 10% health with the smartphone.
  • Allies can be told to Follow or Stay. Leaders can kick members or disband the group.
